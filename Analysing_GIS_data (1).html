CATCHMENT SUMMARY

Southwestern Ghana hosts West Africa's premier auriferous belts (Ashanti, Tarkwaian, and Sefwi). Unregulated riverbed excavator dredging and suction washing have transformed the Pra and Ankobra drainages into hyper-turbid corridors, threatening potable water production for over 1.2 million urban residents.

Selected Target: Southwestern Regional Gold Belt
Primary Basins: Pra (23,200 km²) & Ankobra (8,400 km²)
Geological Domain: Birimian Metavolcanics & Tarkwaian Reefs
Threat Vectors: Turbidity, Mercury (Hg), Arsenic (As)

Geomorphic & Toxicological Mechanics

Mechanisms of Hydroclimatic Degradation

Grounding geochemical transformations and sediment dynamics in peer-reviewed alluvial research and field records.

Elemental Mercury (Hg)

Artisanal miners employ liquid mercury to amalgamate fine gold particles from dredged sluice concentrate. When the amalgam is blow-torched in the open air, vaporized mercury condenses into surrounding waterways. In anoxic riverbed sediments, anaerobic sulfate-reducing bacteria methylate inorganic Hg into bioavailable methylmercury, which biomagnifies up the aquatic food chain into mudfish (Clarias gariepinus) at levels reaching 1.85 mg/kg (3.7× WHO threshold).

WHO Aquatic Limit: 1.0 µg/L · Monitored Offin: 14.8 µg/L

Arsenopyrite Oxidation

Gold in the Birimian Greenstone Belt (Obuasi, Prestea, Bogoso) is locked in sulfide matrices dominated by arsenopyrite (FeAsS) and pyrite. Excavator disruption and dredging churn these previously reducing deeper strata into oxygen-saturated surface waters, triggering acid mine drainage: 4FeAsS + 13O₂ + 6H₂O → 4Fe²⁺ + 4H₂AsO₄⁻ + 4SO₄²⁻ + 4H⁺ This reaction drops river pH, mobilizing dissolved toxic arsenite and arsenate ions up to 820 µg/L.

WHO Standard: 10 µg/L · Kwabrafo Creek: 820 µg/L

Riverbed Incision & Widening

Corroborating findings from Kowalski & Zielińska (2025) across West African alluvial systems, mechanized suction dredges ("changfas") excavate deep riverbed trenches, altering longitudinal hydraulic gradients. Sand extraction rates correlate strongly with channel widening (r = 0.81, p < 0.01) and bank destabilization (r = -0.67). Over-steepened banks collapse during high-flow rain events, tripling bed sediment loads and obliterating benthic macroinvertebrate taxa.

Bed lowering: up to 4.4m · Bank retreat: 8.2m / yr

Municipal Plant Collapse

Conventional water treatment infrastructure (coagulation via aluminum sulfate, flocculation, rapid sand gravity filtration) is engineered for raw water turbidity under 500 NTU. When galamsey drives Pra and Birim river turbidity above 20,000 NTU, settling basins fill with colloidal clay within hours, sand filters blind immediately, and abrasive silica grit shears centrifugal intake impellers. GWCL has been compelled to spend over GH₵ 85M annually in supplementary polymer coagulants.

Daboase Output: 8.5 MGD (Demand: 22 MGD) · Deficit: 61%
